What we do

Turning operations under one roof.

Most turned parts need several operations. Doing them in one setup keeps features concentric and lead times short.

CNC Turning

Outer diameters, steps and profiles turned on CNC for consistent, repeatable results.

Facing

Square, flat end faces that give every part a clean, true reference.

Threading (internal & external)

Standard threads cut on shafts, studs, bores and fittings, checked with gauges.

Grooving

Relief, circlip and O-ring grooves cut accurately to width and depth.

Boring

Internal diameters opened up and finished for bushes, sleeves and housings.

Drilling

Centre drilling and axial holes in the same setup, for true concentricity.

Taper Turning

Tapers and angled features turned to the specified angle and length.

Batch / Repeat Production

Repeat orders run from the same proven program for part-to-part consistency.

Job Work as per Drawing

Send a drawing or sample — we machine to it, using your material or material we source.
