01 Spindle start

The spindle comes up to speed.

CNC turning shapes round parts by spinning the raw bar at a controlled speed while a precisely guided cutting tool removes material along a programmed path. Every diameter, length and shoulder comes from your drawing — and repeats exactly, part after part.

02 Facing & turning

From raw stock to finished part.

First, the end is faced square. Then the tool travels along the bar, bringing each diameter down to size and forming crisp shoulders exactly where your drawing places them.

  • Facing — a clean, square reference face
  • Turning — diameters held to drawing tolerance
  • Steps & shoulders — accurate lengths, sharp corners or radii as specified

03 Threading & grooving

The details that make a part work.

Relief grooves, circlip grooves and threads are cut in the same setup, so every feature stays true to the same axis.

  • Turning
  • Facing
  • Threading
  • Grooving
  • Boring
  • Drilling
  • Taper turning

04 Inspection

Every part checked against your drawing.

Before anything leaves the shop, critical dimensions are measured and compared with your drawing.

  • First-piece inspection before a batch is run
  • Diameters, lengths and threads verified
  • Visual check for finish, burrs and handling marks
  • Any issue flagged to you directly — never hidden
