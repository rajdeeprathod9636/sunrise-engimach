About Sunrise Engimach

A new CNC turning unit, built on precision and honesty.

Founded in 2026 in Rajkot by Shubham Jadav, Sunrise Engimach turns steel and stainless steel components for industrial clients — accurately, cleanly and on time.

Stepped shaft · turned, grooved & threaded

Our story

Started with a simple aim.

Sunrise Engimach is a newly founded CNC turning unit in Rajkot, Gujarat, started by Shubham Jadav with a simple aim: deliver accurate, clean-finish turned components on time, with direct and honest communication.

The workshop is set up for exactly that kind of work — job work and batch production of turned parts in mild steel, alloy steel and stainless steel, with brass and aluminium on request. Every order begins with a careful look at your drawing, and ends only when the parts match it.

We are new, and we say so openly. What we offer in place of a long history is focus: a modern CNC setup, a founder who is personally involved in every job, and a commitment to earn your trust one order at a time.

Why Rajkot

Built in India’s engineering heartland.

Rajkot is one of India’s best-known engineering and manufacturing clusters — home to a dense network of units making auto components, pumps, engines, machine tools, bearings and castings. Being here gives every job we take a strong foundation.

  • Quality raw material, close by

    Steel and stainless bar stock suppliers within the city mean the right grade, sourced quickly.

  • Skilled machinists

    A deep local pool of experienced CNC operators, setters and toolmakers.

  • A complete supply chain

    Heat treatment, grinding, plating and other secondary processes are available locally when your part needs them.

  • Well connected

    Good road connectivity across Gujarat and to the rest of India for dependable dispatch.
